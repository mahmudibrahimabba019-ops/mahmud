How to Order

Fast, secure online ordering — now available 24/7

1

Browse Products

Explore our selection of spices, herbs, yaji blends, superfoods, and seasoning base across the Shop page.

Browse Products
2

Add to Cart

Click "Add to Cart" on any product you wish to purchase. You can add multiple items and quantities.

3

Review Cart

Open your Cart to review items, update quantities, or remove products before checkout.

View Cart
4

Proceed to Checkout

Click the "Proceed to Checkout" button in your cart to start the secure checkout flow.

5

Enter Details

Fill in your delivery information: name, email, phone, and address so we can deliver to you.

6

Place Order

Review the order summary and click "Place Order" to submit your order.

7

Pay Online

Complete payment securely through our integrated payment gateway. We accept major payment methods.

8

Order Confirmation

Receive an order number and confirmation by email. We'll update you about delivery status.

Key Points

  • No more Instagram DMs: Orders are now placed through our online checkout.
  • Delivery Fee: ₦3,000 per order.
  • Secure Online Payment: Payments are processed securely via our payment gateway.
  • Order Confirmation: You will receive your order number and confirmation by email.
  • 24/7 Ordering: Our online shop is open all day, every day.
  • Owner/Admin: Orders can be tracked in the admin dashboard.
